# BCJ Learn — Platform Overview

*What this platform is, who uses it, and how a module goes from idea to a passed quiz and a certificate.*

---

## What is BCJ Learn?

BCJ Learn is BCJ Building Services' internal training platform. It turns the company's standards into structured **modules** that employees learn in a **live seminar**, then prove they understood by passing an **on-site quiz**. Leadership gets live reporting on who has trained, who passed, and who needs help.

![Leadership's live program overview](/docs-img/admin-dashboard.png)

One module is delivered per month (June–October 2026), starting with **Operations & Leadership**.

The platform handles the whole training lifecycle in one place:

1. An **Admin** (or a Department Lead) builds a module: learning content + an AI-drafted quiz.
2. Employees are invited and a **seminar** is scheduled.
3. On seminar day the Department Lead **presents** the material live.
4. When the seminar ends, employees **take the quiz** on their own device.
5. Pass (**85%+**) → they're done and get a **certificate**. Fail → an automatic **retake** is scheduled.
6. Leadership watches it all on **dashboards and reports**.

---

## The three roles

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | BCJ leadership (e.g. Nancy) | Full control: create/manage modules, people, resources, reports, branding, certificate, settings. Sees everything. |
| **Department Lead** | The trainer who owns a module (internally also called "manager"/"teacher" in places) | Builds & **presents** their own modules, reviews questions, sees results for **their** modules only. |
| **Employee** | The people being trained (internally "manager") | Attend the seminar, take the quiz, view resources, track their progress, earn certificates. |

> Roles are set when a person is invited. A Department Lead only ever sees the modules they own; an Employee only sees published modules assigned to them.

---

## Markets

BCJ groups people by their hub **market**: **Atlanta**, **Nashville**, **Charlotte**. Every employee belongs to one or more markets. Markets are used to target who gets a seminar, who a resource is assigned to, and to slice reports.

## The module lifecycle (end to end)

```
Create module ─▶ Add content ─▶ Generate & approve questions ─▶ Choose employees + schedule seminar ─▶ Publish
                                                                                                          │
Employee sees it ◀── Publish makes it live ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
        │
        ▼
Pre-study content (optional, before seminar)
        │
Seminar day: Lead opens check-in ─▶ employees check in (4-digit code) ─▶ Lead presents ─▶ Lead ends session
        │
        ▼
Quiz unlocks ─▶ employee takes quiz ─▶  PASS (85%+) ─▶ certificate, done (annual refresher in 1 year)
                                        │
                                        └▶ FAIL ─▶ retake auto-scheduled ─▶ (up to 3 attempts total) ─▶ 3rd fail = locked, Lead notified
```

---

## Key rules everyone should know

- **Passing score: 85%** (configurable per module). Below that = fail.
- **3 strikes:** an employee gets up to **3 attempts** total (1 first attempt + 2 retakes). After the **3rd failed attempt** the module **locks** and the Department Lead/Admin is notified to coach them.
- **Same questions for everyone:** on the first attempt every employee gets the **same** question set, just in a **randomised order** (and options shuffled). Retakes use the **same concepts, reworded**.
- **Content is open before the seminar:** employees can **preview** a module's learning content any time as pre-study. The **quiz** stays locked until the seminar (check-in + the Lead ending the session).
- **Required resources (acknowledgement):** if a module has linked resources marked "Acknowledgement required," the employee must read and sign them before the **quiz** unlocks.
- **Annual refresher:** after passing, BCJ recommends re-taking once a year; the platform shows a refresher due date.
- **Certificate:** issued automatically on pass; the employee can print it or save it as a PDF. Its wording/signatory is fully customisable by an Admin.

## Language

![Employees switch between English and Español in one tap](/docs-img/emp-language-menu.png)

Employees can switch the whole employee experience between **English** and **Español** from their profile. Quiz questions are translated to Spanish (AI-assisted, reviewed) and shown in the chosen language; if a translation isn't ready, the English version is shown so the quiz always works. Admin and Department-Lead screens are in English.

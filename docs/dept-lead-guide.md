# BCJ Learn — Department Lead Guide

*For the trainer who owns a module: build it, present it live, review questions, and see how your people did.*

> A Department Lead only ever sees and edits the modules they **own**. Trying to open another lead's module by URL returns "Not found." Admins assign module ownership.

---

## 1. Your dashboard

`Dashboard` shows your training at a glance:

- KPIs: **My modules**, **Questions approved**, **Pending review**, **Quiz attempts**.
- **Modules you own** — a card per module with its question-bank progress (e.g. "12/20 approved", an "AI ready" badge if questions are waiting), pass rate, attempts, training day, and quick buttons: **Present in seminar**, **Content**, **Questions**, **See results**.

---

## 2. My Modules

`My Modules` lists the modules you own. From here you can open any of them, or **Add module** (you'll be the owner). Each card has the same quick actions as the dashboard.

### Module detail
Opening a module shows: question bank progress, attempts, pass rate, the **seminar plan** (lessons + total time), a **content tally** (videos/documents/slides/links), the **current roster** (who's expected, with live status), **past deliveries**, and buttons to **review questions** and **see results**.

---

## 3. Building the content

Open **Content** (the lessons builder):

- **Add lesson** — give it a title, description, and duration.
- **Add content** to a lesson — choose **Video**, **Document**, **Slides**, or **Link**. Upload a file (videos up to 2 GB; documents/slides up to 50 MB) or paste a URL.
- **Edit** a content item (title, duration, URL) with the pencil; **reorder** with the up/down arrows; **delete** with the trash.
- **"Don't show on presentation day"** — toggle the eye on any item to hide it from the live presenter (it still stays available to employees as pre-study).
- **Save changes** — an "Unsaved changes" bar appears while you have edits; save to keep them.
- **Version history** — every save is snapshotted. Open **Version history** to see each version with **who edited it** and when, and **Restore** an earlier one (the current content is snapshotted first, so it's reversible).

> Once content is uploaded, the AI can read your videos/documents and use them to draft quiz questions.

---

## 4. Generating & reviewing questions

Open **Questions**:

- **Generate questions with AI** — the AI reads your content and drafts a question bank (first-attempt + retake pools). Review them.
- The bank shows progress: **X approved · Y%**, with counts of Pending / Approved / Edited / Rejected.
- **Filter** by pool (**All / First attempt / Retake**) and status (**Any / Pending / Approved**), and **search** by text. The list is paginated.
- Pick a question from the queue to review it on the right. For each question you can:
  - **Approve** — it goes live.
  - **Edit** — fix the wording/options/correct answer/explanation.
  - **Regenerate with AI** — get a fresh AI draft of that question.
  - **First attempt / Retake** toggle — classify which pool it belongs to.
  - **Duplicate → retake** — create a reworded copy in the retake pool (AI rewords the same concept).
  - **Who answered** — see exactly who has answered this question, right or wrong, with date & time (searchable + paginated).
  - **History** — view past versions of the question and **restore** one.
  - **Reject** — discard it.
- "**Attempts**" and "**Pass rate**" per question tell you how it's performing once people start answering (shows "—" until there are attempts). "**Approved by**" shows who approved it.
- **Publish (X/Y)** — publishes the module so employees can take it. You can also save a module as a **draft** and publish later.

---

## 5. Presenting the seminar

On training day, open **Present in seminar** (full-screen presenter):

1. **Check-in lobby** — you see the roster. **Open check-in**: a **4-digit code** appears for employees to enter on their devices. **Start the session** when you're ready.
2. **Presentation mode** — your content plays as a playlist:
   - The top bar shows the current lesson, an elapsed timer vs. planned minutes, and a progress bar.
   - Navigate with the on-screen **Previous/Next**, or keyboard: **→ / Page Down** next, **← / Page Up** previous, **Space** play/pause, **F** fullscreen, **Esc** to close the outline.
   - Open the **Outline** to jump to any lesson/content item; viewed items get a check.
3. **End the session** — on the last item, tap **End session.** This **opens the quiz** to everyone who checked in ("Session ended — quiz is now open to the room"). You're taken to the results page.

---

## 6. Check-in & scheduling

- **Check-in** is opened from the presenter (above). Each session has a **fresh code**.
- **Schedule / reschedule the seminar** with **date *and* time** from the module's roster section (or the admin sets it up). Rescheduling keeps the same attendees and emails them the new date/time.

---

## 7. Results

Open **See results** for a module:

- KPIs: **Attempts**, **Pass rate**, **Avg score**, **Failed**.
- **Score distribution** chart and **most-missed questions** (top 5 by miss rate) — tells you what to re-teach.
- **Roster — who attended & how they did** — every attendee with their check-in/attempt status; an **Attended** indicator and date/time on attempts.
- **Recent attempts** table (submitted attempts only), with first-attempt vs retake, score, and pass/fail.

---

## 8. My team

`My team` lists the employees trained on your modules (read-only for you):

- KPIs: team members, passed your modules, not yet attempted, at-risk.
- **Search** by name/email/market and **filter by market**.
- Each row shows their status in your modules, best score, attempts, overall status, and last active. You can **send a reminder** (with an email preview) or view a person's detail.

---

## What you can't do (Admin-only)

Department Leads don't manage other leads' modules, the employee directory at large, branding/certificate/email settings, the audit log, bulk user import, or CSV exports. Ask an Admin for those.

3. Delivered beyond the original scope - at no extra cost

A number of features were built that were not in the original scope, to make the platform genuinely easier and safer for BCJ to run:

  • Bilingual - English & Español: the whole employee experience and the quizzes (scope was English-only).
  • Branded completion certificate: issued on every pass, printable, with a fully customisable editor (scope was a simple completion record).
  • Live presenter mode + 4-digit seminar check-in: run the in-person session straight from the platform.
  • Resource hub: shared documents with read-and-acknowledge sign-off and full version history.
  • Audit log: every action recorded and filterable - sign-ins, passes, publishes, reminders and more.
  • Two-step verification: optional 6-digit email code at login for extra account security.
  • Question analytics: see exactly who answered each question right or wrong, with version history and one-click restore.
  • Content version history: every content edit is snapshotted and can be restored.
  • Invited-only access: employees see and take only the modules they have been invited to.
  • Flexible roles: change anyone's role at any time (Employee ↔ Department Lead ↔ Admin) without losing their history.
  • Department Lead workspace: leads see every module, can present or take any, and track their own progress.
  • Smart seminar scheduling: pre-fills who is due and lets you search and add anyone else.
  • In-app Office viewer: Word, PowerPoint, Excel and PDF preview in place - no downloads.
  • Branding controls: platform name, colours, logo and email sender, all editable by Admin.

4. To close out - what we need from BCJ

  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) API key - so the AI question-writing runs under, and is billed to, BCJ's own account. This completes the AI handover.
  • Email-sending domain verification - confirm the sending domain so all automated emails go out from BCJ's address.
  • UAT sign-off - a quick review-and-sign once requirements are met following testing.

# BCJ Learn — Employee Guide

*Everything you need to log in, learn, take your quiz, and earn your certificate.*

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Go to your BCJ Learn link and enter your **work email** and **password**.
   - Use the eye icon to show/hide your password.
   - If your account has **two-step verification** on, after your password you'll get a **6-digit code by email** — enter it to finish signing in. You can tap **Resend code** if it expires (codes last 10 minutes).
2. **Forgot your password?** Click **"Forgot password?"**, confirm your email, and click **Send reset link**. Check your inbox and follow the link to set a new password.
3. After signing in you land on your **Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Your dashboard

Your home screen shows:

- **Your next module** — a large card showing where you stand for the upcoming module (see "Quiz status" below). A **"Open materials"** button takes you into the module to pre-study, with a note like "3 days to training day."
- **Check-in card** — appears on seminar day once your trainer opens check-in (see "Check in").
- **Program progress** — a ring showing how much of the 5-module program you've completed.
- **Your 5-module program** — a row of mini-cards (M1–M5). Each shows a status: a green check if **passed** (with your score), **"Up next →"** for the next one, **"X of 3 attempts left"** if you need a retake, or a lock if you're **out of attempts**.
- **Your recent activity** — your latest passes/attempts.
- **Your stats** — modules passed, average score, your market, and your status.

---

## 3. Modules

Open **Modules** to see the whole 5-module program. You can:

- **Search** modules by name.
- **Filter** with the tabs: **All**, **To do**, **Needs action** (retake or locked), **Passed**.
- Each card shows the module number, title, description, training day, content counts, and a **status badge**:
  - **Available now** — ready to study / take when the seminar opens.
  - **In progress** — you've started.
  - **Retake** — you didn't pass; shows **"X of 3 attempts left."**
  - **Out of attempts** — you've used all 3; your trainer will help.
  - **Passed** — with your score.

### Inside a module

- **Pre-study (before the seminar):** scroll to **"Seminar outline."** Every lesson lists its content (videos, documents, slides, links). The **"Available to preview now"** badge means you can open and study any of it **before** the seminar — tap a item to view it. This is optional but helps you prepare.
- **Required resources:** if you see a yellow **"Sign these required resources before the quiz"** banner, open each listed resource, read it, and tap **"I have read and understood."** The quiz stays locked until all are signed.
- **How this works** card explains that the module is delivered as a live in-person seminar, then you take the quiz right after.

---

## 4. Check in at the seminar

On training day, once your trainer starts the session, a **Check-in card** appears on your dashboard and module page:

1. The trainer shows a **4-digit code** on the room screen.
2. Type the code into the card and tap **Check in.**
3. You'll see **"You're checked in"** — sit tight and listen. The quiz unlocks the moment the trainer **ends the session**.

> If your phone/laptop is dead and you can't check in, you can still be in the room — talk to your trainer; they can open the quiz for you.

---

## 5. Taking the quiz

When the seminar ends, your **Quiz status** card flips to **"Quiz is open."** Tap **Start [Module] Quiz**.

**Intro screen** shows: number of questions, the pass mark (usually **85%**), the time limit (if any), and "Multiple choice." If this is a **retake**, you'll see a note that the questions cover the same material, reworded.

**While taking it:**
- One question at a time. Read it, pick the best answer, tap **Next**.
- A progress bar and a timer are at the top. If it's timed and time runs out, it submits automatically.
- Use **Previous** to go back. The bottom shows how many you've answered.
- On the last question, tap **Submit quiz**.

**Your result:**
- **Passed (85%+):** "Congratulations, you passed!" with your score, confetti, and a **View certificate** button. The next module unlocks next month.
- **Not passed:** "Not quite there" with your score and how many **attempts you have left**. A **retake is automatically scheduled** — review the material and try again. After your **3rd** miss the module **locks** and your trainer is notified to help. You can tap **Review my answers**.

### The 3-attempt rule
You get **3 tries** total per module (1 first attempt + 2 retakes). Retakes cover the **same material, reworded**. After 3 misses the module locks and your Department Lead reaches out.

---

## 6. Reviewing your answers

From a finished attempt (or **My Progress → a quiz**), open the attempt to see a **question-by-question breakdown**:

- Every question is listed, **collapsed**, with a **Correct / Incorrect** marker.
- Tap a question to expand it and see **your answer**.
- If you **passed**, the correct answer is shown on each.
- If you **didn't pass**, the correct answers are **hidden** on the ones you missed — go relearn the material, then retake.

---

## 7. Your certificate

After you pass, open **View certificate** (from the result screen or the module's status card). It's a branded BCJ certificate with your name, the module, your score, and the date. Tap **Print / Save as PDF** to print it or save a PDF copy.

---

## 8. Resources

Open **Resources** for documents your team has shared (policies, guides, SOPs):

- Resources are grouped into **department folders** — tap a folder to open it. Folders that have items you still need to acknowledge open automatically and show a **"X to review"** badge.
- A banner at the top tells you how many resources **need your attention**.
- Tap a resource to read it (PDF, document, video, or text). If it requires acknowledgement, tap **"I have read and understood"** at the bottom.
- A resource can be **updated** — if it changes you may be asked to acknowledge the new version again. New/updated items are badged.

---

## 9. My Progress

**My Progress** is your full training history:

- Top: **modules passed**, **average score**, **total attempts**.
- **Quiz history** — every attempt (newest first, paginated): module, first-attempt vs retake, pass/fail, date & time, score, and a **Review answers** link.
- **Module-by-module** — each module with your latest status and score, or its scheduled date if you haven't taken it yet.

---

## 10. Notifications

The **Notifications** inbox collects your invites, reminders, results, and alerts:

- Filter by **All / Unread / Results / Reminders / Alerts / Invitations.**
- Tap an item to open it (and mark it read). Use **Mark all read** to clear them.

---

## 11. My Profile & language

Open **My Profile** to see your details (name, email, phone, market, title) and your progress stats. Use **Edit profile** to update your info.

### Switch to Spanish / English
In your profile settings there's a **language** toggle — choose **English** or **Español**. The whole employee app (menus, your dashboard, modules, the quiz, resources, progress, your certificate) switches to the chosen language. Quiz questions appear in Spanish when a translation is available; if not, they fall back to English so the quiz always works.

---

## Quick rules recap

- **Pass = 85%** (or whatever your module requires).
- **3 attempts** total; 3rd miss locks the module and your trainer helps.
- **Content is open before the seminar**; the **quiz** opens after the seminar (check in + trainer ends session).
- **Sign required resources** before the quiz unlocks.
- **Certificate** on pass; **annual refresher** recommended.
- Switch language any time in **My Profile**.
